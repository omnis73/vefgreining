A good survey starts with the right questions. I help with the whole process, from defining what to measure to presenting the results to management.

The service falls into a few categories:

Survey design

  • Survey goals defined together with the client
  • Questionnaire written and tested so the questions are clear and neutral
  • Sample and timing planned

Fieldwork

  • Survey set up and sent to customers or employees
  • Response rates monitored and reminders sent
  • Respondents' anonymity protected where appropriate

Analysis and presentation

  • Results analysed by group and compared between periods
  • Reports and presentations for management
  • Dashboards showing trends between surveys

A survey is most valuable when it is repeated the same way and trends are followed over time
