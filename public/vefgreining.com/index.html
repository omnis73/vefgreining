Russia's war against Ukraine in daily data

A free public dashboard following the war day by day. It brings together the Ukrainian General Staff's morning and evening reports with independent sources such as Oryx, ISW and DeepStateMap, and shows clearly which figures come from where.

  • Russian losses
  • Combat engagements
  • Drone and missile attacks
  • Key targets hit
  • Occupied territory
  • Civilian casualties
Open the dashboard Follow updates on Bluesky

Available in English, Ukrainian and Russian, with light and dark modes. Used by more than 21,100 people.
