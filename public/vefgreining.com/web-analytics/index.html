Measuring web performance

Analysis, focused measurement set-up and dashboards for management.

You are probably already collecting all kinds of data. But are you getting the information you need from it? Vefgreining turns data into information you can use to make sound decisions and make better use of the money tied up in your web activities.

The service falls into a few categories:

Preliminary analysis

  • Your web analytics set-up reviewed to see whether anything needs improving
  • An overview analysis of web traffic, focusing on traffic trends and visitors' technology
  • The groundwork laid for measuring results, such as sales, orders, course sign-ups, downloads or article reads

Preliminary analysis lays the foundation for focused work online

Reports and dashboards

  • Long-term trends in traffic and key indicators
  • Daily traffic and results
  • News readership and reading quality
  • Social media results
  • Mailing list results
  • Search keyword performance
  • Sources of online sales

Dashboards bring together information from different systems and present it clearly

Additional services

  • Analysis of your competitive position online and in search engines
  • Setting up and running Google Ads campaigns
  • Managing and sending mailing list emails
  • Managing and posting on social media
  • Reviews of new websites
  • Web consulting

I especially recommend reviewing your competitive position in search engines if you plan to use Google Ads
